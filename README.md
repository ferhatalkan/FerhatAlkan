# Dr. Ferhat Alkan

## Computational Biologist | Postdoctoral Researcher

Welcome to my GitHub profile! I'm a computational biologist specializing in RNA biology, ribosome profiling, and translational regulation in pediatric cancer at the Prinses Máxima Centrum (Princess Máxima Center for Pediatric Oncology) in Utrecht, Netherlands.

### 🔬 Research Focus
- **Ribosome Biology & Heterogeneity**: Investigating specialized ribosome populations and their roles in cancer
- **Microprotein Identification**: Discovering and characterizing microproteins in pediatric cancer using Ribo-seq
- **RNA Interactions**: Large-scale computational prediction and analysis of RNA-RNA interactions
- **Translational Regulation**: Understanding protein synthesis control in cancer cells and immunotherapy
- **CRISPR Systems**: Off-target prediction and optimization for genome editing and therapeutic applications
- **Bioinformatics Tool Development**: Creating open-source software for the research community
