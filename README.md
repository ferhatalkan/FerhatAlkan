# Dr. Ferhat Alkan

## Computational Biologist | Postdoctoral Researcher

Welcome to my GitHub profile! I'm a computational biologist specializing in RNA biology, ribosome profiling, and translational regulation in cancer at the Netherlands Cancer Institute (NKI) in Amsterdam.

### 🔬 Research Focus
- **Ribosome Biology & Heterogeneity**: Investigating specialized ribosome populations and their roles in cancer
- **RNA Interactions**: Large-scale computational prediction and analysis of RNA-RNA interactions
- **Translational Regulation**: Understanding protein synthesis control in cancer cells
- **CRISPR Systems**: Off-target prediction and optimization for genome editing
- **Bioinformatics Tool Development**: Creating open-source software for the research community
